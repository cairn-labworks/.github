# Cairn Labworks

**Useful apps that respect your privacy.**

We build small, focused apps that do one job well. Your data belongs to you.

## Our privacy promise

- **We never sell your data.** Not to advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.
- **We never use your information for ad targeting.** Our apps contain no advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **On-device first.** Your data stays on your phone wherever possible. When an app has to go online, it sends only the minimum it needs, and the app's privacy policy says exactly what that is.
- **No accounts required.** You can use our apps without signing up or giving us an email address.
- **You stay in control.** You decide what gets exported or backed up, and where it goes.
